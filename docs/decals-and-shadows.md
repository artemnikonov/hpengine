# Decals and actor shadows

Decals (scorch marks, `ectoMark`, and the blob shadows under characters) are not drawn as sprites. A decal actor is attached to a BSP surface by `ADecal::execAttachDecal`, which stores a record in the surface's `Decals` list. At draw time `URender::DrawFrame` walks each drawn surface, clips every record to the polygons of that surface with `URender::ClipDecal`, and draws the remaining pieces right after the surface. Actor shadows are decals created by a script (`ActorShadow.Update`) that the renderer itself triggers. Core owns the whole chain; the frontend draws the finished pieces.

## Attaching: `ADecal::execAttachDecal` (Engine.dll 0x10410770)

The body was read from disassembly; the decompile confuses stack slots here.

- Gate 1 (0x1041081d..0x1041085a): `Level->Engine->Client` exists and `Client.Decals` is on. A decal without a Texture logs "No Texture" and fails.
- The ray starts at the actor's Location and ends at `Location + (-XAxis) * Distance`, where `XAxis` is the X axis of the decal's Rotation (0x10410a30..0x10410a7b).
- `UModel::LineCheck(..., ExtraNodeFlags = 6)` is a polygon trace over the model (6 is NF_ShootThrough | NF_NotVisBlocking). A miss or `Item == -1` rejects.
- The original ignores the trace's own hit location. It takes the node's surface, recomputes the point as the ray's intersection with that surface's plane (0x10410b45..0x10410bd5), then calls `UModel::FindCoplanarSurface(Item, Point, 0)` (0x10410bfa) and recomputes the point again with the chosen surface's plane (0x10410c82..0x10410ce8). The chosen surface can belong to a neighbouring node of the same plane, and its PolyFlags are the ones that matter.
- Rejections after that: `(XAxis | N) == 0` (0x10410d47; the dot is inlined in x, z, y order); `|(Base - Point) | N| > 0.001` (0x10410dbc, components stored to float first); and `Surf.PolyFlags & 0x600` (0x10410dd0). Those two bits are the texture's auto-pan bits `bAutoUPan`/`bAutoVPan` (PolyFlags bits in declaration order of class Texture: bInvisible 0, bMasked 1, bTransparent 2, ... bTwoSided 8, bAutoUPan 9, bAutoVPan 10), not Invisible or FakeBackdrop: a decal does not stick to a surface whose texture moves by itself.
- The record (0x10410dd9..0x104113cd): the `FDecal` (0x40 bytes: four Vertices, Actor at +0x30, Nodes at +0x34) is inserted before the first record with the same texture (`InsertZeroed`), else appended; the surface is added to the decal's `SurfList`. Radius is `sqrt(DS^2 * USize^2 * 0.5)`; the basis is rotated 45 degrees for a given direction and straight for a random or degenerate one (random branch uses the render `rand()` stream); vertices are in world space until the end, when they are made relative to the surface's `pBase`.
- `FDecal.Nodes` (`FUN_104127c0`, 0x104127c0): the surface nodes the record is not clipped to nothing against, found by a 2D clip with "edge cross normal" planes.
- `MultiDecalLevel` wrap (0x104113d2..0x10411e6a): a grid of record points of that size, each traced +-50 along the normal; a neighbouring surface with `|cos| > 0.7` gets its own record with the vertices projected on its plane.
- Detach: `execDetachDecal` (0x10412ef0) walks `SurfList` from the end and removes the actor's first record.
- `Surf->Decals` is rebuilt per model; `FBspSurf.Nodes` is filled by `UModel::PostLoad` (nodes in order, `Surfs[node.iSurf].Nodes`). A mover brush tracker's surfaces are inserted into the level model by the tracker, so the same trace finds a mover fragment as a level node and the record lands on the tracker surface. A tracker surface's `Nodes` list is empty, so `DrawDecals` clips the record against every drawn fragment of the surface.
- Precision: the live caller runs in the render window (24-bit x87), where every operation rounds to float. Outside it (game tick) the body is not ported and fails loudly; the campaign never calls it there.
- Port: [`HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.DecalSurface.cs`](../HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.DecalSurface.cs) (surface selection and gates), [`HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.DecalStore.cs`](../HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.DecalStore.cs) (records, nodes, wrap, detach), [`HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.AttachDecal.cs`](../HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.AttachDecal.cs) (the native entry). The Core body was compared bit for bit with the original's own body over 60 cases on each of three maps.

## Actor shadows

- `Engine.ActorShadow.Update(Actor L)` is script bytecode. It returns early if the owner is missing, deleted, or `Owner.Style == 3` (translucent). It copies the owner's Opacity, takes `Loc = Owner.BonePos(Owner.BoneName(0))` with Z raised by 1, and does nothing more while the owner moved no more than `MoveThreshold` (and, if oriented, kept its rotation). Otherwise it calls `DetachDecal()`, sets `ShadowDir`, sizes `DrawScale = Max(Extent.X, Extent.Y) * ShadowSizeFactor / Max(Texture.USize, Texture.VSize)` from `GetRenderExtent`, places itself at `Loc` with `Rotator(-ShadowDir)`, and calls `AttachDecal(MaxShadowDist, Vector(Owner.Rotation))`.
- Nothing in the campaign scripts calls `Update`. The renderer does, from Render.dll inside `URender::SetupDynamics` (call at 0x10b304be, the compiler inlined the `ADecal::eventUpdate` wrapper, so there is no import or call to its body). It reads `Actor->Shadow` (+0x258) and runs `Update` with the argument `L` hard-wired to None. So the "no light passed" branch is the only live one: the shadow direction is straight down, `(0,0,-1)`.
- `SetupDynamics` order is actor by actor: brush branch, sprite setup, then shadow. The shadow's `Update` calls `BonePos`, hence `ApplyAnim(rootOnly)`, and sees the skeleton header from before the frame's `GetFrame` (D-58).
- Presentation: the shadow actor has Texture `ShadowT` and Style 4 (Modulated). `DrawDecals` maps Style 4 to `0x40000040` and Style 3 to PF_Translucent. The decal path never reads Opacity (the only Opacity read in `DrawFrame` is the sprite one); `ClipDecal` reads only ScaleGlow (+0x17c) and AmbientGlow (+0x18c), and D3DDrv does not apply that light to a modulated decal: vertex colour is 0xFFFFFFFF and the blend is DESTCOLOR/SRCCOLOR.

## Drawing: `DrawFrame` decal loop and `ClipDecal`

- Gate (0x10b26390..0x10b263af): the device is not SpanBased, `Client.Decals` is on, and the surface has no `PolyFlags & 0x8000000` (mirrored). With the software driver there are no decals, as in the original; the game picks Direct3D on first run.
- The decal list of a surface is drawn right after its surface (`DrawComplexSurface`, DrawFrame 0x10b26334, loop 0x10b26390..0x10b263a8), in the surface's own pass, before that group's actors. There is no test for map surfaces: a mover brush tracker surface takes its own flags, so a decal on a mover is drawn in the mover's pass. The frame carries the surface's PolyFlags and its texture's PolyFlags (+0x80) for tracker surfaces.
- Every record is clipped by `ClipDecal` against each polygon of the surface drawn in this frame (`FSavedPoly`, the output of `ClipBspSurf`, only built on a non-SpanBased device), using that frame's camera. Each frame of the tree does this, so a decal can be drawn in a mirror and in the master frame; a piece names its frame and the frontend draws it only in that view.
- A record with a non-empty piece sets `Actor.LastRenderedTime` (+0x260) to `Level.TimeSeconds` (+0x3b4). This is game state, which is why decals are computed in Core. The original does not check whether the decal actor is hidden.
- `URender::ClipDecal` (Render.dll 0x10b27060, thunk 0x10b01082), read from disassembly because the decompile of the point-insert block is garbled: the decal rectangle is built from the surface normal (normalised when |n|^2 is at least 1e-8) and the four corners raised along the normal, the polygon points are taken to world space through Uncoords, and the rectangle is cut by planes through the viewer and each polygon edge. Edge crossings insert a point, points on the negative side are removed, and the rest get U along V1-V0 and V along V3-V0 in DrawScale units. Static buffers of 24 vertices are used. All x87 operations are in the render window, so float arithmetic matches.
- Device texture scale: the U/V factor is `1/(USize * DrawScale)` (D3DDrv `SetTexture` divides by `(2^mip * USize_mip) * UScale`, with `UTexture::Lock` setting `UScale = USize/USize_mip * DrawScale`).
- Port: [`HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UEDecalClip.cs`](../HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UEDecalClip.cs) (ClipDecal; compared with the original's body over 339 frames), `DrawFrameDecals` in [`HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.AttachDecal.cs`](../HP1.Core/Runtime/World/UELevelRuntime.AttachDecal.cs), and the group rule in [`openhp1/RenderRules.cs`](../openhp1/RenderRules.cs) (`DecalGroup`).
